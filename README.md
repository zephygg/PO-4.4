About the project

AquaHydro is a new way to do greenhouses - with the help of our little friends, fish!
The fish release ammonia with their lifecycle, which can be fed to plants as a fertilizer, giving us both a beautiful natural synergy and a pretty sight!
And with AquaHydro, you can automate and monitor your greenhouse - plants, and fish. With our sensors, you can track soil and water quality, temperature, humidity, and see them in your app from your phone.
The database tracks all of your recent readings, so you can see the statistics change over time.
